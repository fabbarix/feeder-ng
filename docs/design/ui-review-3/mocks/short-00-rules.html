Short viewports and fixed chrome: one rule set

Size classes are read from the viewport, height first. "Short" is a height under 500 px (phone landscape, an on-screen keyboard in a resizing browser, 200–400 % zoom). Green marks what changes. Budget: fixed and sticky chrome covers at most 35 % of the height at load; a sheet's body keeps at least 6 rem and 40 % of its panel.
