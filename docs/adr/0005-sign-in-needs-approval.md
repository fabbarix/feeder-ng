# Sign-in needs a User's approval, not just a Google account

Feeder stays Google-only, but a Google account is no longer enough, and an Admin is no longer the only gate. Someone signs in straight away if their address was added to the Household (it is on the allowlist, or it is the email of a Member). Anyone else with a *verified* Google email creates an **Access Request** and waits on a page that shows nothing about the Household; they get no session and no data until **any signed-in User** approves it (one approval is enough; approving adds a non-Admin User). A denial removes the request and the address may ask again after a cool-down. We considered keeping the Admin-only allowlist (rejected: a family member should be able to let in a relative without finding the Admin) and open sign-up (rejected: the Household's data, including names, schedules and photos, must stay private). Because strangers can now write to the database before they are trusted, requests are rate-limited per IP and per address, capped globally, expire after 14 days and are audited. This extends the access rules of spec §2; the allowlist remains the list of Users, and Admin-only powers (managing Members, Meal Slots, Shops, the allowlist and settings) are unchanged.
