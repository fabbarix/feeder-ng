# Cool-down and Variety Rules give way when too few Recipes fit; the Course is still planned

Status: **Accepted** (2026-11-11). Amends spec §7.2, §7.3, §7.4 and decisions 22 and 23 ([functional-spec](../spec/functional-spec.md), decision 30). Plan: [crs-plan.md](../dev/design/crs-plan.md).

Until now Cool-down and Variety Rules were filters: a Recipe inside its Cool-down, or one that would break a Variety Rule, was never a candidate, and a cell with nothing left was **Couldn't plan** (Cool-down or Variety Rule). With Courses this became the common case, not the edge case: a Household with three Sides and seven dinners would see "Couldn't plan: every Recipe is in Cool-down" on most nights, and a new Household with five Recipes sees it from day six. The user's words: "Cool downs and variety do not apply when there are enough recipes to do the rotation, as the user adds recipes the variety and cool downs will start having an effect." The second half gives the meaning: while a Household has **too few** Recipes to keep them, Cool-down and Variety Rules give way; as the Household adds Recipes, they hold again. That is the rule this ADR implements.

1. **Two kinds of rule.** *Hard* rules are never broken by the planner: Course fit, Exclusions of the Members who eat that Course, the Advance Step lead time, one Recipe per slot and date, the Whole meal held back beside a kept Course, Use-by (for Leftovers) and a Slot Rule's narrowing. *Soft* rules are **Cool-down** and **Variety Rules**. Dislikes and Favourites are not filters at all: they stay score terms, as before.
2. **Strict first, then the least bad.** For each cell the planner first looks for candidates that keep every rule, exactly as before; if there are any, it picks the best-scoring one and nothing changes. Only when the soft rules leave **no** candidate does it make a **relaxed pick** among the Recipes that pass the hard rules, in a fixed order (spec §7.2, step 2b): a Recipe not reserved for a later Slot Rule before a reserved one; one outside its Cool-down (only a Variety Rule in the way) before one inside it; among those inside, the longest gap to that Recipe's nearest fresh cook in any Course, then the fewest days of its Cool-down left; then the fewest Variety Rules broken; then the usual score; then the lowest Recipe id. So the rules still shape the plan: the pick is the Recipe that breaks them least.
3. **No Couldn't plan for Cool-down or Variety Rule.** A required Course with at least one Recipe that passes the hard rules is always filled. The planner now applies the hard filters before the soft ones when it counts eliminations, so a Recipe counts under Cool-down or Variety Rule only if it passed every hard filter, and such a Recipe is always a relaxed candidate. The two causes are therefore **unreachable**, not kept for an edge case: the planner never records them, Couldn't plan drops their tiles and the **Review Cool-down** and **Review Variety Rules** fixes, and the stored counts `coolDown` and `varietyRule` stay in the JSON (always 0 on new reasons) only so an old cached SPA still parses them. The remaining causes are Nobody attends, No Recipes, No Recipes for this Course, Slot Rule unmet (its Recipes are all excluded or too late for an Advance Step), Excluded, Advance Step too late and No fit.
4. **Rules bite again on their own.** A relaxed pick is stored as such on its Planned Meal (`planned_meals.relaxed`). Every time the planner replans, **every** unpinned fresh Planned Meal it would keep (relaxed or not) is checked before the walk, against the Cool-down and Variety history with its own cook left out (spec §7.2, *The keep check*): if it keeps the soft rules it stays and is not relaxed; if it breaks one and its cell has a strict candidate (the Household added Recipes, or the older cook is now more than a Cool-down away), the planner replaces it; otherwise it stays and is marked relaxed, with a chip computed then. So the plan does not churn, and the why chip is always true: a Planned Meal kept from a strict pick that a later Pinned Planned Meal pushed inside its Cool-down says so. A Pinned Planned Meal is the User's choice and is never relaxed.
5. **Optional Courses are never filled by a relaxed pick** (ADR 0008, point 7): "Dessert when there is a fresh one, otherwise none" is what optional means. Leftovers are not affected: Leftover first never looked at Cool-down or Variety Rules and still comes before any fresh pick.
6. **Slot Rule reservation stays.** It still keeps a Slot Rule's only Recipe out of earlier cells where the strict Cool-down would block the rule's cell, so the rule's cell gets its Recipe with a strict pick whenever possible. In a relaxed pick a reserved Recipe is the last resort (point 2). Decision 22's "Pinned wins" changes in two places. (a) When a Pinned or Cooked Planned Meal of the rule's only Recipe already sits inside the rule cell's Cool-down, the rule cell is **filled by a relaxed pick** of that Recipe (it meets the rule; it comes round sooner) instead of staying Couldn't plan. (b) When two open Slot Rule cells reserve the same Recipe inside each other's Cool-down, the earlier cell gets it with a strict pick and the later cell gets a **relaxed pick** of it, with its relaxed chip ("Again soon: had Tue"), instead of staying Couldn't plan. In both cases the cell is Couldn't plan only when the Recipe fails a hard filter (an Exclusion, or too late for an Advance Step).
7. **Swap** lists strict alternatives first, under the usual scoring; when there are fewer than 3, it fills the list with relaxed ones in the relaxed order, each with its chip, for every Course (optional ones included: the User is choosing). A reserved Recipe is still never offered. Choosing one pins it, as any Swap.
8. **Determinism.** The planner stays a pure function of its Input: the relaxed order is total (it ends on the Recipe id) and the only random term is the seeded jitter inside the score, as before.
9. **How a relaxed pick is shown.** The "best available" chip was dropped in QA round 1 ([ui-fix-plan §6](../design/ui-fix-plan.md)) because it said nothing: "no reason is better than a non-reason". A relaxed pick therefore says **what** came round sooner, with the fact that makes it so, never a vague quality word: "Again soon: had Tue", "Again soon: also on Thu" (Cool-down; the nearest fresh cook of that Recipe, before or after) and "3rd fish this week", "fish two days running" (Variety Rule; the Tag and the count or the adjacent day). It is a neutral why chip (never the warn or danger tone): the plan is fine, just less varied. It is not the day tile's chip unless the Planned Meal has nothing else to say, it is always in the Planned Meal sheet, and the sheet adds one line that turns it into the action the user's rule implies: "Few Side Recipes, so this comes round sooner. Add Side Recipes for more variety." with **Mark Recipes as Side** (the same Recipes-list filter as the "No Recipes for this Course" fix) or, for the main Course, **Add Recipes**.

**Considered options**

- *A default Cool-down per Course* (the earlier open question 1 and spec §7.6: Settings > Courses sets, say, 1 day for Sides): fixes Sides only, needs a setting the Household must discover and tune, and a Household with few Mains still sees Couldn't plan. Rejected; §7.6, task CD and migration 1420 are withdrawn.
- *Relax only for Courses other than the main Course*: keeps the old behaviour for single-Course Households, but the user's rule is about having too few Recipes, which is just as true of a new Household's Mains. Rejected: the rule applies to every Course, so this changes single-Course Households too.
- *Relax Exclusions too* when nothing else fits: an Exclusion is an allergy or a hard ban. Rejected.
- *Keep Cool-down and Variety Rule causes for an edge case*: there is none left once hard filters are counted first (point 3); keeping dead tiles would only confuse. Rejected.
- *Show a warn-tone chip or a Couldn't plan-like notice for relaxed picks*: it would make the most common state of a young Household look like a problem. Rejected for a neutral chip plus the sheet line.
- *Replan relaxed picks every time*: lets newly added Recipes in at once, but the jitter seed changes between replans and the plan would churn. Rejected for "replace only when a strict candidate exists" (point 4).

**Consequences**

- Migration 1400 (team A) also adds `planned_meals.relaxed INTEGER NOT NULL DEFAULT 0 CHECK (relaxed IN (0,1))` in its rebuild; `plan.Meal` gains `Relaxed bool`; the planner's `ExistingMeal` and `Placement` gain it (team B).
- Two new why-chip codes, `cooldown_relaxed` (`date`: the nearest fresh cook; `recipeId`) and `variety_relaxed` (`tag`, and `value`: the count this week, or `date`: the adjacent day), phrased by the SPA; `alternativeOut` gains `relaxed`. `best_available` is retired: the planner still emitted it for a Planned Meal with nothing to say (`internal/planner/why.go`), and team B removes it, so no chip code is sent then.
- Couldn't plan loses two causes and the Cool-down fix (team G2); the Admin-only list loses Cool-down.
- An upgraded Household that showed Couldn't plan (Cool-down or Variety Rule) sees those cells filled after the startup replan; the CHANGELOG says so (team H).
